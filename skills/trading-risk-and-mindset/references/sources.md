# Sources

The ideas in this skill are paraphrased, not quoted. These are the places to go deeper. Each line says what this skill takes from it.

## Risk and position sizing

- **Van K. Tharp, *Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom* (2nd ed., 2006)** and ***The Definitive Guide to Position Sizing* (2008).** R-multiples, expectancy, and the case that position sizing drives most of the variation in results.
- **Ralph Vince, *The Mathematics of Money Management* (1992).** Optimal f, and why sizing at the growth-optimal fraction produces brutal drawdowns.
- **Edward O. Thorp, "The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market" (1997 paper; also *A Man for All Markets*, 2017).** Kelly sizing and the case for fractional Kelly.
- **Jack D. Schwager, *Market Wizards* (1989) and the later Wizards books.** The recurring theme across very different traders: control the loss on every trade, and size so you can survive being wrong.

## Psychology and decision making

- **Mark Douglas, *Trading in the Zone* (2000).** Probabilistic thinking: any trade can lose, the edge is in the series, and accepting the risk in advance removes the fear that causes hesitation and rule-breaking.
- **Brett N. Steenbarger, *The Daily Trading Coach* (2009), *Enhancing Trader Performance* (2006), *The Psychology of Trading* (2003).** Self-coaching, journaling, solution-focused review (study what you do well), and emotions as signals.
- **Annie Duke, *Thinking in Bets* (2018).** "Resulting": judging a decision by its outcome. Separating decision quality from luck.
- **Denise Shull, *Market Mind Games* (2012).** Using emotions as data rather than suppressing them.
- **Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011); Kahneman and Tversky, "Prospect Theory" (1979).** Loss aversion: losses feel roughly twice as strong as equal gains, which drives holding losers and cutting winners.
- **Hersh Shefrin and Meir Statman, "The Disposition to Sell Winners Too Early and Ride Losers Too Long" (Journal of Finance, 1985); Terrance Odean, "Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses?" (1998).** The disposition effect, documented in real brokerage accounts.
- **Brad Barber and Terrance Odean, "Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth" (Journal of Finance, 2000).** The most active traders in their sample earned the least after costs: evidence against overtrading.
- **Peter M. Gollwitzer, "Implementation Intentions" (American Psychologist, 1999).** If-then plans make intended behavior far more likely under pressure.
- **Atul Gawande, *The Checklist Manifesto* (2009).** Short checklists at decision points catch errors experts make under stress.
