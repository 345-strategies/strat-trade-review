## Every trade

- Entry: the trigger, the break of the prior bar's high or low. Not chased past ____R.
- Stop: far side of C1. Entered with the order, or an alert I act on at first touch.
- Size: from the calculator, rounded down.
- First target: nearest magnitude or level at least 1R away; if none, no trade.
- Management: ______ at T1, stop to breakeven, runner trails under each completed ____ bar.
- Adds: only on a new trigger in my direction, total risk kept at 1R or less.

## Pre-trade checklist (all yes, or no trade)

- [ ] I can name the combo and timeframe: ______
- [ ] Continuity agrees (or I am at half size)
- [ ] Stop at C1, size from the calculator, risk is 1R or less
- [ ] Target at least 1R away
- [ ] I am under my daily limit and max trades
- [ ] One word for how I feel right now: ______. If it is "rushed", "bored", "angry" or "need to make it back", I wait one bar.
